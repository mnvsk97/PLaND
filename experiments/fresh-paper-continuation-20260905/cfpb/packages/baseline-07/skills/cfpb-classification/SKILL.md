# Cfpb Classification SOP

1. [S01] Classify the complaint into the financial-product category provided by the company whose conduct is challenged. Prefer the most specific supported product, including a product identifiable from the respondent's role or specialization. Reporting by a creditor about its own card or loan remains that card or loan product; reporting by a debt collector remains Debt collection. Choose Credit reporting or other personal consumer reports for a credit bureau or consumer-reporting service, not simply because a consumer discusses a report, identity theft, or the FCRA. Identify the underlying service first, then apply the product definitions. <!-- pland:english -->
2. [S02] Read the complete evidence text supplied in this request and separate the consumer's concrete account or transaction facts from copied legal arguments, generic dispute templates, and requested remedies. Identify the named provider, the kind of account or agreement, what happened, and which service the consumer wants corrected. Give those factual details more weight than repeated terms in long statutory quotations. A loan or card account does not become a credit-reporting product merely because the consumer wants its entry deleted; a prepaid card does not become a transfer service merely because its funds were moved. Use a provider's stated role or clearly identifiable product specialization when the narrative omits the generic product name. Treat redaction placeholders as missing information rather than as evidence for a category. <!-- pland:english -->
3. [S03] Select exactly one allowed label using these product definitions. Checking or savings account: maintaining a bank deposit account, its balance, access, fees, overdrafts, checks, or debit-card servicing. Credit card: a revolving card account, purchase-charge dispute, balance transfer, interest, billing, rewards, or card payment. Credit reporting or other personal consumer reports: a credit bureau or reporting service's files, inquiries, investigation, disclosure, or correction of information. Debt collection: a third-party collector, collection demand, debt validation, collection lawsuit, or garnishment. Money transfer, virtual currency, or money service: executing, receiving, reversing, tracing, or releasing a remittance, wire, electronic money transfer, payment-processing service, digital wallet, or virtual-currency transaction, including a transfer performed by a bank. Mortgage: a home-secured loan and its origination, servicing, escrow, or foreclosure. Payday loan, title loan, personal loan, or advance loan: personal or installment borrowing, cash advances, payday borrowing, or borrowing secured by an already-owned vehicle's title. Prepaid card: a gift, reloadable, stored-value, payroll, or benefit card and access to its funds. Student loan: education borrowing and its servicing. Vehicle loan or lease: financing or leasing a vehicle purchase, vehicle payments, or repossession. Choose the product responsible for the disputed service, not a different product mentioned only as a destination of funds or background context. Generic references to fraud, credit reports, legal rights, or a bank do not themselves determine the label. <!-- pland:english -->
4. [S04] Before returning the label, check that it describes the disputed financial product rather than merely a law, symptom, or requested remedy. If choosing Credit reporting or other personal consumer reports, verify that the complaint concerns a bureau or reporting service rather than the named lender's loan, card, or a collector's debt. If choosing Money transfer, virtual currency, or money service, verify that executing or processing the transfer is itself disputed rather than merely the way funds left a prepaid card or deposit account. For a purchase-charge or billing dispute, distinguish a credit-card borrowing account from a debit card tied to an explicitly identified deposit account; the word bank alone does not establish the latter. Resolve the choice using the narrative's strongest product evidence, then return exactly one JSON object shaped as {"label":"<known bucket>"} with no explanation or additional fields. <!-- pland:english -->
