# Cfpb Classification SOP

1. [S01] Identify the requested item and classify the supplied consumer complaint by its financial PRODUCT, not by its issue, remedy, or quoted law. First identify the company or service being complained about and its relationship to the consumer: original lender, debt collector, credit bureau, deposit bank, card provider, or money service. A request that a lender remove its own loan from a credit report is still a complaint about that loan product; a request that a collector remove a collection account is Debt collection. Reserve Credit reporting or other personal consumer reports for a complaint about the bureau or reporting service itself, or a reporting-only narrative with no identifiable product or collector. Use product context and provider identity throughout the narrative, including headings and salutations; do not let generic FCRA, privacy, identity-theft, or report-deletion language override an identifiable product. <!-- pland:english -->
2. [S02] Read the complete evidence text supplied in this request. <!-- pland:english -->
3. [S03] Classify the evidence into exactly one known bucket: `Checking or savings account`, `Credit card`, `Credit reporting or other personal consumer reports`, `Debt collection`, `Money transfer, virtual currency, or money service`, `Mortgage`, `Payday loan, title loan, personal loan, or advance loan`, `Prepaid card`, `Student loan`, `Vehicle loan or lease`. Identify the financial product and provider whose conduct is disputed, not merely a legal citation or an incidental payment method. A complaint about an identified mortgage, education loan, vehicle financing, credit card, or personal/payday/installment loan keeps that product category even when it mentions credit reporting or collection. Use Debt collection when the central conduct is a debt collector's demand, debt validation, collection lawsuit, or garnishment; use Credit reporting or other personal consumer reports when the central target is a credit bureau, report, inquiry, or reporting service rather than an identified lender's product. Gift cards, stored-value cards, payroll cards, and benefit-disbursement cards belong to Prepaid card, including disputes about funds or transfers from those cards. Distinguish a bank deposit account's servicing, debit-card purchases, or charge disputes from a remittance, payment-processing, digital-wallet, or virtual-currency service. Use the whole narrative to resolve ambiguity and select the most supported category; do not infer a credit card solely from a purchase dispute or a bank account solely from the word account. <!-- pland:english -->
4. [S04] Return exactly one JSON object shaped as `{"label":"<known bucket>"}`. <!-- pland:english -->
